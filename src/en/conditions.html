Article 04

Conditions applicable to Investors

4.1 KYC

Every Investor must complete the identity verification process (KYC) operated by SEMOA before making their first investment. This process requires the submission of valid identity documents.

4.2 Investment

The Investor may invest in the published Projects up to the amounts displayed on the Platform. The minimum investment is 10,000 FCFA, unless otherwise stated on the Project page.

4.3 Irrevocability

Any validated investment is irrevocable. The Investor may not withdraw their funds before the Project's maturity date, except where the fundraising fails (in which case a full refund is made).

4.4 Repayment

At the Project's maturity, SEMOA repays the capital plus the ROI calculated on a pro rata temporis basis, directly to the Mobile Money account registered by the Investor.

4.5 Fees

KPITAL charges Investors no registration fee and no commission on gains. Standard Mobile Money fees charged by the Investor's operator apply to transactions.

Article 05

Conditions applicable to Project owners

5.1 Submission

Submitting a Project entails acceptance of the 4-step validation process (scoring, field visit, expert committee, security deposit). KPITAL reserves the right to reject any Project without having to justify its decision.

5.2 Truthfulness

The Project owner warrants the accuracy and truthfulness of all the information and documents provided. Any falsification results in the immediate termination of the contract and may give rise to legal proceedings.

5.3 Security deposit

Before any disbursement, the Project owner provides a security deposit to SEMOA in accordance with the agreed terms (real estate, third-party guarantee, institutional co-signature). This security may be enforced in the event of default.

5.4 Commission

KPITAL charges a commission of 4% on the total amount of a successful fundraising, collected by SEMOA before the first disbursement. An additional 5% goes to the shared reserve fund managed by SEMOA.

5.5 Reporting

The Project owner undertakes to provide a monthly progress report accompanied by field photos, failing which ongoing disbursements may be suspended.

Article 06

Risks and warnings

Investing on the Platform involves risks, including:

  • The partial or total loss of the capital invested in the event of a Project owner's default;
  • The lack of liquidity: funds are locked until the Project's maturity;
  • Market risk: economic conditions may affect the Project's performance;
  • Default risk: the Project owner may be unable to repay Investors.

The protection mechanisms (escrow, reserve fund, security deposit) reduce these risks but do not eliminate them. The returns shown are estimates and do not constitute a guarantee. Past performance is not indicative of future performance.

You are advised never to invest more than you are prepared to lose and to diversify your investments.

Article 07

Escrow and management of funds

Investors' funds are held in dedicated escrow accounts for each Project, opened and managed exclusively by SEMOA. KPITAL has no unilateral access to these funds.

The funds of the shared reserve fund are also managed by SEMOA and are subject to a quarterly independent audit, the results of which are published on the Platform.

In the event of KPITAL's closure or cessation of activity, SEMOA continues to administer the accounts in accordance with the agreements in force and makes repayments to Investors on the scheduled maturity dates.

Article 08

Fee structure

KPITAL's fee structure is as follows:

  • Investors: Free registration. No commission on gains. The operator's Mobile Money fees are payable by the Investor.
  • Project owners: Free submission. Commission of 4% on a successful fundraising. Contribution of 5% to the SEMOA reserve fund. As a result, 91% of the amount collected is paid to the Project owner.

KPITAL reserves the right to amend its fee structure with 30 days' notice given to registered users.

Article 10

Termination and suspension

KPITAL may terminate or suspend any account in the event of: a breach of the Terms of Use, fraud, provision of false information, or conduct harmful to the Platform or its users. Termination is notified by email with 5 business days' notice, except in cases of urgency.
