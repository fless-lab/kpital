The project

Villa Tokoin is a 4-bedroom masonry home, built on land title no. TG-LOC-2024-0781, in the residential district of Tokoin Ouest in Lomé. The property is fully furnished and equipped. An institutional tenant (an international NGO) has signed a firm 24-month lease, with the monthly rent paid into the dedicated escrow account.

The financing covers the final renovation works (plumbing, air conditioning, exterior painting) and the funding of the rental deposit. The project owner, Agbeko K., holds the land title and has already repaid two real estate projects on the platform.

The ROI of 11%/yr is calculated on the net rent (gross rent minus charges) relative to the amount raised, over 24 months.
