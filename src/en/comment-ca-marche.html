KPITAL connects investors with project owners verified on the ground. No project goes live without passing every level of validation. Here is exactly how it works.

Selection process

Four levels
of validation

No project goes live without passing every step. The rigour of the application file is the investor's first guarantee.

  1. 01

    Submission & automatic scoring

    The project owner completes a structured application: legal information (RCCM business registration, land title or lease), bank statements from the last 6 months, business plan and project documents. The algorithm calculates a score based on financial history, sector and location. Applications below the 60/100 threshold are rejected automatically.

  2. 02

    On-the-ground verification by an agent

    A KPITAL agent visits the site in person within 5 business days. Geolocated photos, verification of the land title, the trade register and the project's real-world existence. No project goes live without this visit. Free for the project owner.

  3. 03

    Validation by the expert committee

    Three independent experts (finance, sector, legal) validate the application and the stated return. They are publicly identified and all of their opinions are published. It is their reputation on the line, not just ours.

  4. 04

    Publication with a visible A–D rating

    Before going live, the project owner places a deposit with the escrow partner and a dedicated escrow account is created. The project is published with its rating from A (low risk) to D (high risk). Score, visit report, committee opinion and key documents: everything is visible.

Investor journey

Investing, in five steps

From opening your account to repayment. No registration fees, no commission on your gains: KPITAL charges its commission to the project owner.

  1. 01

    Create your account

    Enter your name, your Mobile Money number (T-Money or Flooz) or your bank details, plus a piece of ID. Identity verification (KYC) is carried out by our licensed payment partner, the trusted third party that secures your funds.

    2 min · KYC
  2. 02

    Explore the projects

    Browse the available projects and filter by sector, duration, ROI and A–D risk rating. Each listing contains the on-the-ground visit report, the legal documents and the expert committee's opinion.

    Filters · Full transparency
  3. 03

    Invest the amount you choose

    From 10,000 FCFA via T-Money, Flooz or local bank transfer. For the diaspora, payment can also be made by SEPA or SWIFT to the project's dedicated escrow account, with a unique reference in the subject line.

    T-Money · Flooz · Transfer · SEPA/SWIFT
  4. 04

    Track progress

    Your dashboard shows the status of each project, the milestone-based disbursement schedule, on-the-ground follow-up photos and monthly reports. A notification each time a milestone is reached.

    Dashboard · Monthly reports
  5. 05

    Receive capital + ROI

    At maturity, the capital and ROI are credited to your available KPITAL balance. You receive 100% of the stated ROI. You can then reinvest or withdraw to Mobile Money or a bank account.

    Available balance · Reinvest or withdraw
