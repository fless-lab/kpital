Investor

8 questions
What is the minimum amount to invest?Popular

The minimum ticket on KPITAL is 10 000 FCFA for the vast majority of projects (Tier 1, 2, 3 and 5). For Tier 4 projects (SME expansion), the minimum is raised to 25 000 FCFA.

There is no imposed maximum. You can invest as much as you wish in a single project, or spread across several projects to diversify.

This low threshold is deliberate: KPITAL is designed so that everyone can invest, not just large fortunes.

Can I invest in several projects at the same time?

Yes, without limit. Your dashboard consolidates all your active investments, the amounts, the maturity dates and the expected ROI. Diversifying across several projects and several tiers is the recommended strategy to reduce risk exposure.

How does KPITAL make money?Popular

KPITAL charges a 4% commission on each successful fundraise, paid by the project owner. Not by investors. You receive 100% of the ROI announced on the project page.

There are no registration fees, no withdrawal fees, and no commission on your gains as an investor.

How is my ROI calculated if I invest while a fundraise is underway?

The ROI is calculated pro rata temporis from the effective date of your investment until the project's maturity date. The project page always shows the remaining duration and the corresponding ROI.

Example: a project at 18%/year, you invest with 3.5 months remaining → your net ROI = 18% × 3.5/12 = 5.25% on the amount invested.

Can I withdraw my money before the end of the project?

No. Once invested, your capital is locked in a dedicated escrow account until the project's maturity. Investing on KPITAL is a commitment for the duration of the project.

This is why we recommend investing only amounts that you will not need during the period concerned. The duration of projects is always clearly displayed before investing.

A secondary market (resale of shares) is planned for a future version of the platform.

What happens if a project does not reach its fundraising target?

If a project does not reach its minimum target within the allotted fundraising period (generally 45 days), the fundraise is cancelled. Your money is fully refunded to your Mobile Money by our licensed payment partner, with no fees and no additional delay.

What information is available on each project before investing?

Each project page includes: a risk score and an A–D rating, a field visit report with geolocated photos, the opinions of the three committee experts, verified legal documents (RCCM, deed/lease), a summarised business plan, the type of guarantee deposited, the disbursement schedule, and the owner's history if they have already run a project on KPITAL.

How am I kept informed of the project's progress over its duration?

You receive SMS and email notifications at every key milestone (disbursement validated, monthly report published, any incidents). Your dashboard shows the status of each project in real time, along with field photos and the monthly reports submitted by the owner.

Project owner

6 questions
What type of project can be submitted on KPITAL?

KPITAL accepts projects in five categories: rental real estate (Tier 1), commerce & stock (Tier 2), seasonal agriculture (Tier 3), SME expansion (Tier 4), and community projects with social impact (Tier 5).

The project must be operational or in an advanced start-up phase. Ideas with no verifiable physical reality are not eligible.

How much does KPITAL take from my fundraise?Popular

KPITAL charges 4% of the total raised once the target is reached. This deduction is carried out before the first disbursement. Investors pay no commission. It is entirely at your expense.

In addition, 5% of the amount raised feeds the common reserve fund. You therefore receive 91% of the amount raised (100% − 4% commission − 5% reserve), distributed in progressive instalments according to the milestones.

What documents must I provide to submit a project?

Mandatory documents: ID, RCCM or professional licence, land title or lease agreement, bank statements (last 6 months), business plan or financial forecast, and a detailed description of the project with a precise address.

Recommended documents: current photos of the site, client contracts or letters of intent, references from prior activity.

How long does the validation process take?

The full process takes on average 12 to 15 business days:

Immediate automatic scoring → field visit within 5 days → expert committee within a further 5 days → guarantee deposit with the escrow partner and fundraise opening within 3 days. If a file is incomplete, the timeline is extended.

How are the funds paid to me once the fundraise is reached?

The funds are paid in progressive instalments tied to milestones defined in your file. You submit a progress report with photos for each milestone. The next instalment is released within 48h.

The funds are paid to the bank account or Mobile Money provided when you registered.

Progressive disbursements protect both investors and your reputation in the event of unforeseen difficulties.

My project was rejected. What can I do?

You receive a rejection report detailing the blocking points. The most frequent causes: a score below 60/100, incomplete or invalid documents, a project that cannot be physically verified, or a lack of sufficient guarantee.

You can resubmit in 3 months after addressing the points identified. No penalty is applied for new submissions.

Security & risks

6 questions
What happens if a project owner disappears or defaults?Popular

Three mechanisms trigger simultaneously. First, the funds still in escrow are not released: the owner loses access to the instalments not yet paid out. Second, the guarantee deposited by the owner is enforced and paid back to investors. Third, the reserve fund steps in to cover up to 50% of the remaining loss.

Our licensed payment partner manages these three triggers and keeps you informed at every stage.

Can I lose my entire capital?

Yes, in theory. Any investment carries a risk of capital loss. The protection mechanisms (escrow, reserve, guarantee) significantly reduce this risk but do not eliminate it entirely, particularly for high-risk projects (Rating C or D).

KPITAL recommends diversifying your investments across several projects and several tiers, and never investing funds you might need in the short term.

What happens if KPITAL shuts down or goes bankrupt?

Your funds are never in KPITAL's accounts. They are in the dedicated escrow accounts for each project. If KPITAL shuts down, our licensed payment partner continues to administer the accounts according to the established contracts, and processes refunds at the normal maturity.

This is precisely why we structured the relationship with our payment partner as an independent third party.

Who holds the money and the escrow?

The funds are held and managed by a payment institution licensed by the BCEAO (the UEMOA monetary authority), as an independent third party to KPITAL. KPITAL never directly handles investors' money.

How is the A–D risk rating assigned?

The rating results from four inputs: the algorithmic score (financial history, sector, geography), the field agent's report, the expert committee's opinions, and the type of guarantee deposited. Each criterion is weighted and documented. The scoring algorithm is not published in full to prevent abusive gaming, but all the criteria are listed in the project owner documentation.

Are there projects that never pass validation?

Yes. Projects that are systematically rejected: projects with no verifiable physical existence, owners with unresolved default records, sectors with high regulatory risk (currency exchange, speculation), and projects whose announced ROI is unrealistic given sector data. KPITAL publishes the acceptance rate of applications every quarter.

Payment & withdrawal

5 questions
Which mobile operators are accepted to invest?

KPITAL accepts T-Money (Togocel) and Flooz (Moov Africa) via a Mobile Money aggregator (T-Money, Flooz). A single payment point for both operators. You choose the one you prefer for each transaction.

How and when do I receive my refund?

On the project's maturity date, the refund is triggered automatically: capital + ROI calculated pro rata temporis, directly to your registered Mobile Money number. Processing takes 24 to 48h business hours after the maturity is validated.

Are there fees on transactions?

Your operator's standard Mobile Money transaction fees (T-Money or Flooz) apply at the time of payment. KPITAL itself charges no fees on investor transactions. The refund is processed with no additional fees.

Can I change my Mobile Money number after registration?

Yes, from your account settings, in the "Payment information" section. The change is subject to a light KYC re-verification (identity confirmation by SMS + photo). The change takes effect within 24h.

Is ROI taxable in Togo?

Financial income from investments is in principle subject to income tax in Togo. KPITAL provides an annual statement of your gains to make your tax return easier. Consult a tax advisor or the OTR to find out your exact personal situation.

Diaspora

4 questions
I'm in France / Germany. Can I invest on KPITAL?Popular

Yes. KPITAL is open to diaspora investors from France, Germany, Belgium, Canada and the United States. International opening is planned from July 2026 with the launch of the platform.

Join the waiting list to be notified as soon as it opens and to get priority access to the pilot projects.

How do I send money from abroad to invest?

Two options are available. For small amounts: via Mobile Money if you have an active Togolese number (T-Money or Flooz). For larger amounts: an international SEPA transfer (Europe) or SWIFT directly to the dedicated escrow account of the selected project. The escrow account details are provided when your investment is validated.

How can I receive my refunds if I'm in Europe?

The refund is made via the payment method you used to invest. For SEPA/SWIFT transfers, the return transfer is made to the same source IBAN. You can also choose to leave the funds in your KPITAL account to reinvest them in an upcoming project.

Are the interface and support available in English?

Yes. The platform is available in French and English. Customer support responds in both languages. You can switch the language from the menu at the top right (FR / EN) or from your account settings.
