Our mission

Togolese savings
deserve better

KPITAL connects money sitting idle in banks to real projects that create value in Togo. Verified on the ground, secured by a dedicated escrow account. From 10,000 FCFA.

Hundreds of billions of FCFA sit idle in bank savings at 3% while serious entrepreneurs lack the capital they need. KPITAL is the bridge.

In Togo, access to financing remains the main obstacle to the growth of SMEs and project owners. Banks demand collateral that is out of reach. Tontines cannot scale. And household savings earn almost nothing.

On the other side, tens of thousands of Togolese, in Lomé, in the regions and across the diaspora, have money to invest but do not know where, or have no access to serious opportunities.

KPITAL closes this gap. A verified, transparent platform, secured by a dedicated escrow account. Real projects, inspected on the ground. Democratized access to returns between 8 and 28% per year.

Why KPITAL

A real problem,
a serious solution

Three simple, mutually reinforcing observations that no Togolese bank solves today.

01

Access to credit is broken

Fewer than 15% of Togolese SMEs have access to formal bank credit. Rates are prohibitive, collateral impossible. Serious entrepreneurs go under for lack of 500,000 FCFA at the right moment.

02

Savings are not working

Bank deposits earn 2 to 4% per year in Togo, often less after inflation. Money loses value while real opportunities exist ten minutes away on foot.

03

The diaspora wants to invest, not give

Togolese in France, Germany and America send billions home every year. Many want to invest wisely back home but have neither the tools nor the confidence to do so.

Our values

What
we believe in

01

Total transparency

Every published project comes with its field visit report, the expert committee's opinions, the risk rating and verified legal documents. You know everything before you invest.

02

Independent custody

Your funds never pass through KPITAL's accounts. Every investment goes into a dedicated escrow account for the project. On repayment, capital and ROI appear as an available balance, managed by our licensed payment partner.

03

Real accessibility

10,000 FCFA is the minimum ticket. Mobile Money or local bank transfer depending on your profile. No finance degree required. KPITAL is built for everyone, not just high net worths.

04

Measurable impact

Every financed project creates jobs, generates local income and strengthens Togo's economic fabric. We publish impact data at the end of each project.

The 5-year vision

KPITAL aims to become the reference infrastructure for crowdfunding in French-speaking West Africa. Not just a platform. A system: scoring, field agents, escrow, wallet, impact reports.

An entrepreneur in Kpalimé looking for 800,000 FCFA should be able to raise it in 15 days, with the same standards as a European platform. A Togolese in Paris who wants to invest 50 € back home should be able to do it in three clicks.

That's what we're building.
