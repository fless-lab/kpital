Selection process

Four levels
of validation

No project is published without clearing every stage. Rigour is our guarantee.

01

Submission & automatic scoring

The project owner completes a structured application. The algorithm calculates a score based on financial history, sector, geographic area and the documents provided. Applications below the threshold are rejected automatically.

02

On-site verification by an agent

A KPITAL agent visits the project in person. Geotagged photos, verification of the land title, trade register and bank statements. No project is published without a visit.

03

Expert committee validation

Three independent experts (finance, sector, legal) validate the project and the stated return. They are publicly identified; their reputation is on the line, not just ours.

04

Publication with a visible A–D rating

Each project is rated from A (low risk) to D (high risk / strong return). Score, visit report, committee opinion, key documents. Everything is visible. Nothing is hidden.

Security

3 protection
mechanisms

Your capital is protected on several levels, independently of KPITAL.

01

Dedicated escrow

Your money is locked in a dedicated escrow account per project. Neither KPITAL nor the project owner can access it alone. Released gradually in validated tranches.

Independent of KPITAL
02

Reserve fund

5% of every fundraise feeds a shared reserve fund. In the event of partial failure, the reserve covers up to 50% of the loss. Managed by our licensed payment partner, audited quarterly.

Audited every 3 months
03

Owner deposit

Every project owner posts a guarantee before accessing the funds. An asset, a third-party endorsement, or a co-signature. Legally recorded through our licensed payment partner. The owner cannot disappear without consequences.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you
need to know

Can I invest from any mobile operator in Togo?
Yes. KPITAL accepts T-Money (Togocel) and Flooz (Moov Africa) through a licensed Mobile Money aggregator. A single payment point for all operators.
What happens if a project fails?
The reserve fund (5% of every fundraise) steps in and covers up to 50% of your loss. The deposit posted by the project owner is also triggered. You are notified immediately.
Does KPITAL take a stake in the projects?
No. KPITAL charges a 4% commission on each fundraise to the project owner. Your capital and your ROI belong entirely to you.
How is my money secured between my investment and the repayment?
Your money is locked in a dedicated escrow account for the project. A separate account for each project. Neither KPITAL nor the owner can access it alone.
What level of maturity is required to submit a project?
Your project must be operational or in an advanced start-up phase. We verify the legal and financial documents and the physical existence of the project before any publication.
Can I invest in several projects at the same time?
Yes. You can diversify your portfolio across as many projects as you like, with a minimum of 10,000 FCFA per project.
