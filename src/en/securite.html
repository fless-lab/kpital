02
Reserve fund

5% of every raise, set aside for you

For every franc raised on the platform, 5% automatically feeds a shared reserve fund managed by SEMOA. This fund does not belong to KPITAL: it is there for you.

5% deducted automatically from every successful raise, before disbursement to the project holder.

Coverage up to 50% of the loss in the event of partial failure or default by the project holder.

Managed by SEMOA, not by KPITAL. KPITAL has no unilateral access to this fund.

Audited every quarter. The audit report is published on the platform. Total transparency.

In the event of partial default by the project holder

The reserve fund covers up to 50% of your loss. Automatically triggered on a SEMOA decision. Immediate notification.

03
Project-holder guarantee

A legal guarantee before any access to funds

Before a single franc is disbursed, the project holder posts a formal guarantee with SEMOA. This guarantee is legally tracked: the holder cannot disappear without legal consequences.

Three accepted forms: a property placed under mortgage, the surety of a solvent third party, or the co-signature of an institution.

Notarized deed via SEMOA. The guarantee is legally registered, not just a verbal commitment.

Activation in the event of default. If the project holder fails to repay, the guarantee is enforced to reimburse investors.

Visible on every project page. The type of guarantee (not its exact value) is displayed publicly.

Accepted guarantee types
Real estate property

Placed under mortgage via a SEMOA notarized deed. The most common option.

Surety of a solvent third party

A personal or institutional guarantor co-signs the debt. Verified by SEMOA.

Institutional co-signature

A bank or microfinance institution co-signs the commitment. Required for Tier 4+ projects.

The type of guarantee is shown on every project page. The precise value is not disclosed publicly for reasons of legal confidentiality.

Risk management

Understanding the
risk levels

All investment carries risk. KPITAL does not promise guaranteed returns. We give you the tools to assess the risk and protect you in the event of default. Each project is rated from A to D based on its profile.

Rating A · Low risk
Tier 1 rental real estate
Tangible, verifiable asset. Stable rental income. Real estate guarantee. Assessment score > 80/100. ROI 8–14%/year.
Rating B · Moderate risk
Trade & agriculture
Depends on market cycles and the season. Personal or third-party guarantee. Score 65–80/100. ROI 15–24%/year.
Rating C · High risk
Tier 4 SME in expansion
Strong growth potential but operational uncertainties. Enhanced monitoring required. Score 60–65/100. ROI 18–25%/year.
Rating D · Very high risk
Speculative or emerging projects
Reserved for experienced investors. High ROI but capital not guaranteed even with the protection mechanisms. Higher minimum ticket.

Investing carries risks, including the partial or total loss of the capital invested. Past performance is no guarantee of future performance. KPITAL puts protection mechanisms in place but does not guarantee full repayment in the event of a major default by the project holder. Only invest amounts you can lock up for the duration of the project.
