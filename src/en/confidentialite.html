Article 01

Collection of personal data

KPITAL collects personal data in connection with the use of its platform. This data is collected fairly and transparently.

Data collected at registration

  • Last name, first name, email address, phone number
  • Identification information (identity document for KYC)
  • Banking information (Mobile Money)

Data collected during use

  • History of investments and transactions
  • Interactions with the platform (logins, page views)
  • Technical data (IP address, browser, device)

The collection of this data is necessary to provide our services and to ensure the security of transactions.

Article 02

Use of personal data

Your personal data is used to:

  • Create and manage your user account
  • Process your investments and transactions
  • Verify your identity (KYC procedure)
  • Contact you regarding your investments
  • Improve our services and the security of the platform
  • Respond to your requests and complaints
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

We use your data only for the purposes described above and do not process it for any other purpose without your prior consent.

Article 03

Sharing of personal data

KPITAL does not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share your data in the following cases:

  • With SEMOA: For the management of payments and escrow
  • With the authorities: Where required by law or court order
  • With our service providers: For technical services (hosting, security)
  • With your consent: In other specific cases

All our partners are subject to strict confidentiality obligations.

Article 04

Data security

KPITAL implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against loss, unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure.

These measures include:

  • Encryption of sensitive data
  • Strict access controls
  • Continuous monitoring of systems
  • Regular security audits
  • Staff training

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Article 05

Your rights regarding your data

In accordance with data protection law, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: To know the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification: To correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure: To delete your data under certain conditions
  • Right to restriction: To restrict the processing of your data
  • Right to portability: To receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to object: To object to the processing of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@kpital.finance.

Article 06

Data retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected:

  • Account data: For the duration of your registration plus 5 years after closure
  • Transaction data: 10 years for tax and accounting obligations
  • KYC data: 5 years after the end of the relationship

After these periods, your data is deleted or anonymized.

Article 07

Contact and complaints

For any question regarding this privacy policy or your personal data, contact our data protection officer:

Email: privacy@kpital.finance
Address: KPITAL Finance SARL, Lomé, Togo

You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.
