Join KPITAL

We are building the infrastructure that lets anyone invest in Togo's real economy, from as little as 10,000 FCFA. If building something useful, rigorous and grounded in the real world speaks to you, let's talk.

Why us

What drives us

Tangible impact

Every line of code, every field visit, every approved application funds a real activity: a home, a harvest, a business. Not speculation.

Rigor as a culture

Trust is earned through proof: scoring, physical verification, independent committee, escrow. We publish nothing we have not verified. It is demanding, and it is the job.

Building for Togo

A local team solving local problems, with international standards. You learn fast, you take on real responsibilities early.
